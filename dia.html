How the diamond RWA flow works

Step 1 · Acquire low-value diamonds

We start from low-grade lab-grown or industrial diamonds whose market value is limited and tends to decline due to oversupply. Each sample receives a unique ID and baseline measurements (color, clarity, size).

Step 2 · Heat treatment

Controlled high-temperature annealing is performed under specific atmospheres. The goal is to modify defect structures and color centers without damaging crystal integrity.

Step 3 · Electron-beam processing

Selected samples are irradiated with ≈10 MeV electron beams. This creates or rearranges color centers in the diamond lattice. Dose, energy and temperature profiles are logged for every stone.

Step 4 · Screening & certification

Stones with promising color transformation are screened and 6–8 of them are sent to accredited gemological labs for professional grading and certification.

Step 5 · NFT minting on Cardano

Certified stones are linked to NFTs that store treatment history, grading results, and high-resolution images as on-chain metadata. Optional physical delivery is provided for Korean NFT holders who want to verify the stones directly.

Step 6 · RWA framework

The outcome is not just a collection, but a reusable framework for diamond-backed RWAs: from depreciating LGD to value-enhanced, science-verified, on-chain assets.

Research plan & feasibility

Feasibility

Value Enhancement and Advanced Industrial Application Research for Diamonds
The project focuses on converting low-value diamonds into higher-value colored stones through controlled treatments, while also building data for future semiconductor, quantum, and sensor applications.

Timeline (example)

  • October 2025 – Color-transformation research on industrial diamonds.

Why this research matters

  • Upgrades low-value diamonds into higher-value colored stones.
  • Builds fundamental data on diamond defects and color centers.
  • Supports future applications in jewelry, quantum sensors, power electronics, thermal management and more.
  • Connects physical material science with verifiable Cardano RWAs.

Machines & facilities

Electron beam (~10 MeV)

Experiments are planned using high-energy electron beam facilities such as the Advanced Radiation Research Institute in Jeongeup (Korea Atomic Energy Research Institute), which is capable of delivering ≈10 MeV beams for material processing.

This infrastructure allows precise control of irradiation dose and temperature, which is essential for defect engineering in diamond and for reproducible color-enhancement research.
